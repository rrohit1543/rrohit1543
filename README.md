## 🧠 About Me

I’m an **AI Engineer and Researcher** with a BS-MS in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from **IISER Bhopal**.

My work sits at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Generative AI, Multimodal Learning, and Real-Time Systems**.

I’m particularly interested in building intelligent systems that can **understand human movement, language, and interaction**, with a strong focus on **sign language technology, gesture recognition, motion analysis, and AI-generated avatars**.

Currently, I work on **LLM training, evaluation, data quality, and multimodal AI workflows**, while continuing to explore research and product development in computer vision and generative AI.
